# Network view (tickets #9 and #10)

The picture of the query and its matches is the interactive SVG in `src/NetworkView.tsx`. Its geometry, the hand-worked pixels and the limits are in [network-layout.md](network-layout.md); the model it draws is in [data-contract.md](data-contract.md) ("Network model and expansion"). The small picture of ticket #11 and its `src/layout.ts` are retired.

## What the picture shows

- Three columns: the query, its shown matches (the same matches, order, identity, type and Best flags as the list below, at the same minimum identity), and, after an expand, the matches of those matches.
- A chip per entry: a database badge with the database name as text, then name, native id and identity with type. The outline shape also carries the database (CARD rounded, NCBI cut corners, ResFinder pill). The query is a larger chip.
- Best is a ribbon reading "Best"; in a group whose top identity is below 90% it reads "Highest identity shown" (the same rule as the list: the model's weak flag, `isWeakGroup`). A step-1 match under 90% that is not the top of its group has a plain "Below 90%" ribbon (the list's "Below 90% identity: weak evidence of equivalence." line, also in its tooltip and accessible name): never Best, never Highest identity shown, which stay with the group's top match only. Step-2 chips carry no ribbon: their flags are relative to the entry they are a match of, not to the query.
- Edges are directed from the query. RBH: solid, arrowheads at both ends (a reciprocal pair). OWH: dashed, one arrowhead (the reverse direction is not implied). The identity is in a pill on every step-1 edge.
- Long names and ids are cut with an ellipsis (19 characters for a name, 24 for an id, 16 for the query name); the full text is in the tooltip and the card.

## Interaction

- Every chip is a focusable button (`role="button"`, `tabindex="0"`, an accessible name with entry, database, identity, type and Best). Click or Enter makes it the query: the parent pushes one history entry, so Back and Forward step through the explored entries (the slider and the database filter keep `replaceState`). Focus moves to the new query chip. A database filter that would hide the clicked entry is cleared.
- Hover or focus shows a tooltip (`role="tooltip"`, `aria-describedby`; lines from `src/networkTooltip.ts`), highlights the node, its edges and its card in the list; hovering or focusing a card highlights its node. Escape dismisses the tooltip. The tooltip is hoverable (WCAG 1.4.13): the pointer can move from the node onto it, and it closes 250 ms after the pointer has left both (`HOVER_GRACE_MS`). It sits below the node, above it, or beside it, and stays inside the figure. When the box scrolls sideways the tooltip follows its node, and it is hidden while its node is scrolled out of the box (before #25 any sideways scroll dismissed it, so a node that had to be scrolled into view by hover or Tab showed no tooltip). L on a node moves focus to its card; "Show in network" on a card focuses its node.
- A "+n" pill on a match is a separate button ("Expand matches of X (n)", `aria-expanded`); it adds that entry's own matches in a third column ("Matches of a match"), dashed and lighter, each labelled "match of X" on its chip. The caption says a match of a match is not a match of the query. A step-2 hit that is also a match of the query is not a new node: a lighter link between the two existing nodes is drawn and labelled "match of a match". Step-2 edges carry no identity label. Two matches that are RBH with each other and both expanded give a step-2 edge each way; they are drawn as one line with two arrowheads and the caption counts them (`visibleEdges().merged`). One-way hits in both directions stay as two lines. The "match of a match" labels are slid along their curve to the first of t = 0.5, 0.4, 0.6, 0.3, 0.7, 0.2, 0.8 that touches no other edge or label (docs/network-layout.md, "Label placement"); in a crowded picture some contact remains. The reverse hit from an RBH match back to the query (expected from reciprocity) is hidden and counted in the caption. Expansion is React state: not in the address, reset when the query changes. The 25 node cap shows one line of text (`network-cap`).
- The figure keeps its design width inside its own box (`overflow-x: auto`, `tabindex="0"`, labelled) so a node stays at least 44 CSS pixels at any width; the page itself does not scroll sideways. From 1152 px the picture sits in the sticky left rail with the comparison strip (#25, ADR 0006); the one-step picture fits the rail (tested), and the expanded three-column picture scrolls sideways inside its box. An expand scrolls the box to its right end (instantly under reduced motion) so the new column or link is in view, the box always draws a scrollbar (14 px, in tokens, a macOS overlay one would be hidden) and shades the edge that has more to scroll to, and the notes that qualify the picture (match of a match, hidden reverse hits, the node cap) sit above it. Below a 640 px box the picture is drawn portrait instead (next paragraph).
- Portrait (a phone, a box under 640 px, owner decision: a vertical stack): the query chip across the top, the step-1 matches in one column below it with their identity pills on the lines that connect them, and the step-2 nodes of an expanded match indented directly under it. It is the same picture with the same behaviour: a click or tap makes a node the query (one history entry), the tooltip shows on hover and keyboard focus, node and card highlight each other, the "+n" pill expands and collapses, Best, Highest identity shown and Below 90% are drawn as in landscape, a match of a match is dashed and the caption says it is not a match of the query, and the 25 node cap message is shown. It is drawn at the box's width, never scaled, so nothing scrolls sideways, a chip is 52 and a pill target 44 CSS pixels high, and the box is not a Tab stop; Tab goes down the picture (a match, its pill, its own step-2 nodes, the next match). Text is cut earlier on a narrow chip (`slot.limits`, never longer than in landscape). Positions: `docs/network-layout.md`, "Portrait". On a touch screen a tap makes the node the query at once, so the tooltip (which a tap shows only for a moment) cannot be the way to read a full id: the full id and name of every match are on its card in the list below the picture ("Show in network" and L pair them), and a step-2 node's full id is in its accessible name; after a tap it is the query and its panel shows everything. The portrait tooltip says so ("Tap or press Enter to make this the query. Its full id is on its card in the list below"). A resize across 640 px redraws the picture and keeps the query and the expansion.
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`: no entrance animation or transition.

## Comparison strip bars (#20, ADR 0006)

The bars in the at-a-glance strip have their own single mapping, `scale(value, width = 200)` in `src/bars.ts`: `length = min(max(value, 0), 1) x W` with W = 200 px, and `overflow` when the value is above 1 at the displayed precision (`round(value x 1000) > 1000`), so a coverage printed as 100.0% never gets the mark. A coverage over 100% is drawn full length with a chevron, and its exact percentage is printed beside it.

| Value | Arithmetic | Length (px) | Overflow |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | 0 x 200 | 0 | no |
| 0.5 | 0.5 x 200 | 100 | no |
| 0.9 (weak line) | 0.9 x 200 | 180 | no |
| 1.0 | 1.0 x 200 | 200 | no |
| 1.3 (a coverage) | min(1.3, 1) x 200 | 200 | yes |

The weak-evidence mark is a dashed `--danger` line at `scale(0.90)` = 180 px, labelled "90%" above the bar, on the identity bar only. It is an interpretation, not a filter. The minimum identity is not drawn on the bars: every shown bar is at or above it by definition, so a line for it repeated the slider and was read as a second threshold (owner decision, 2026-11-11). The line sits on a 5 px `--surface` halo, so it is read against `--surface` (4.5:1 for `--danger`) where it crosses the bar fill. Hand-worked values: `tests/fixtures/README.md`, "Bar scale"; tests: `src/bars.test.ts` and `tests/comparison.spec.ts`.

Layout of a strip row (visual polish, 2026-11): the database chip and its Best or Highest identity shown label share the heading; the match count is its own muted line under it, so a long label never pushes the count to a different place in another row; tied top matches are separated by a rule; the 90% key glyph is aligned to the first line of its text.

## Bars in Match details (#23)

Each Match details card draws the same bar next to its identity and coverage numbers: the shared `Bar` component (`src/Bar.tsx`, also used by the strip), so one `scale()` decides every length. A Best badge sits on the identity line beside the value; Highest identity shown stays on a line of its own. A coverage above 100% gets the full bar, the chevron and "(over 100%)"; the 90% mark is not repeated in the cards (the "Below 90% identity" line and the Best or Highest identity shown labels are unchanged). The fill is `--label` on a `--panel` track, and the cards sit on `--panel`, so the tested pairs `--label` on `--panel` (3:1) and `--control-border` on `--panel` already cover them. Tests: `src/Bar.test.ts`, `tests/cards.spec.ts`.
